About chimiNES

chimiNES is a browser-based NES emulator interface powered by JSNES. Import a .nes ROM you have the right to use, then play with the arrow keys, Z (A), X (B), Enter (Start), and Shift (Select).

Imported ROMs are saved locally in this browser. Game audio is played through your browser's Web Audio support. Themes, pixel filters, and CRT mode customize the interface and display.
